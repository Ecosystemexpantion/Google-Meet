EEM26 Cast

A small free Android app that shares your phone screen (and microphone) with everyone in your live class. Only the presenter needs it.

⬇ Download EEM26 Cast (Android)
  1. Tap the download button above, then open the downloaded file.
  2. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser (Settings opens automatically) and tap Install.
  3. Go back to the presenter studio, tap Share screen → Open EEM26 Cast → Start sharing.
  4. Android asks once to start recording or casting: choose Entire screen and tap Start.
  5. To stop: tap Stop presenting in the studio, or Stop in the EEM26 Cast notification.

The app only talks to your class server. It isn’t on the Play Store, which is why Android shows an “unknown app” warning.
