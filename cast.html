EEM26 Live for Android

Run your whole class from your phone: the presenter studio, built-in screen sharing, chat, polls and every host control in one app. It updates itself — you only download it once.

Download EEM26 Live
  1. Tap Download, then open the downloaded file.
  2. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser, then tap Install.
  3. Open EEM26 Live → Start teaching and sign in. Schedule a class or start one now.
  4. Tap Share screen, choose Entire screen and tap Start. Switch to any app — the class keeps running.

The app isn’t on the Play Store, which is why Android shows an “unknown app” warning. Turn on Do Not Disturb before presenting so notifications don’t appear on your shared screen.
