EEM26 Live for Android

Run your whole class from your phone: the presenter studio, built-in screen sharing, chat, polls and every host control in one app. Attendees can join from it too.

Download EEM26 Live (Android)
  1. Tap the download button above, then open the downloaded file.
  2. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser and tap Install. (Already have EEM26 Cast? This replaces it.)
  3. Open EEM26 Live → Start teaching, sign in and tap Start the class.
  4. Tap Share screen. Android asks once to start recording or casting: choose Entire screen and tap Start. Then switch to any app — the class keeps running.
  5. To stop: Stop presenting in the studio, or Stop sharing in the notification.

The app only talks to your class server. It isn’t on the Play Store, which is why Android shows an “unknown app” warning. Turn on Do Not Disturb before presenting so notifications don’t show on your shared screen.
